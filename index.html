About BASU PENS

Delivering quality writing instruments with innovation, reliability, and a strong commitment to Nepalese manufacturing.

A Trusted Nepalese Stationery Manufacturer

At BASU PENS, we believe every great idea begins with a simple stroke of a pen. Writing is more than a daily activity—it is a powerful way to express creativity, knowledge, and innovation.

Our journey began with a vision to manufacture high-quality writing instruments that combine comfort, durability, affordability, and modern design. Every product is developed with attention to detail to ensure smooth ink flow, consistent performance, and an enjoyable writing experience.

Whether for students, professionals, educators, or creative individuals, BASU PENS is committed to producing dependable writing solutions that support learning, productivity, and everyday communication.

Beyond manufacturing quality products, we are dedicated to promoting Nepalese industry through continuous innovation, responsible production, and customer satisfaction.

The vision behind BASU PENS and our commitment to quality manufacturing in Nepal.

Founder & CEO

Mr. Dwarika Prashad Joshi

BASU PENS

Building Nepal's Future Through Local Manufacturing

BASU PENS was founded with a vision to establish a trusted Nepalese stationery manufacturing company capable of producing high-quality writing instruments for both national and future international markets.

Under the leadership of Mr. Dwarika Prashad Joshi, the company has focused on quality, innovation, affordability, and customer satisfaction while encouraging local manufacturing and industrial development within Nepal.

His leadership philosophy emphasizes discipline, continuous improvement, ethical business practices, and long-term sustainable growth rather than short-term success.

Our Vision

To become one of Nepal's leading stationery manufacturers by delivering innovative, affordable, and environmentally responsible writing products while creating opportunities for local employment and industrial growth.
